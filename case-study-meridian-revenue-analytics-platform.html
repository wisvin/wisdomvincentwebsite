The brief

A Series A SaaS company needed a revenue analytics platform their finance team could use without engineering support. The brief: MRR tracking, ARR forecasting, churn analysis, and a clean executive dashboard.

02 /

The plan I drafted

  • Agree the handful of KPIs finance actually uses
  • Put them at the top, always visible
  • Add charts for trend, breakdown and retention
  • Make exporting reports one click
03 /

The process, step by step

Complete analytics platform UI with a dark sidebar, KPI summary bar (MRR, ARR, churn rate, LTV), interactive chart area, cohort retention table, and a pipeline forecast section with visual breakdown.

  1. Data Architecture Planning

    Mapped the KPIs the finance team cared about most: MRR, net ARR, churn rate, LTV, and expansion revenue. Built component hierarchy around these.

  2. Dashboard Layout

    Dark sidebar with icon navigation. Main content area with sticky KPI cards at top and chart modules below. Fully responsive down to tablet.

  3. Chart Components

    Line chart for MRR trend, stacked bar for revenue breakdown by segment, and a heat-map style cohort retention grid.

  4. Export & Reporting

    PDF export button wired to browser print API. CSV export modal for raw data download.

04 /

The bottleneck

! bottleneckToo many numbers, not enough signal

Finance wanted every metric, but a dashboard showing everything makes it hard to see what changed.

05 /

How I fixed it

✓ fix

Key KPIs sit in fixed cards at the top and the detail lives in separate chart modules below, so the headline story is clear in seconds.

06 /

The result

  • Finance team adopted the platform on day one — no training required
  • Monthly reporting time reduced from 6 hours to under 30 minutes
  • Investor board deck now pulls directly from the dashboard KPIs
