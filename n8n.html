What is n8n?

Open-source automation at the level your business actually needs

n8n is a powerful open-source workflow automation platform. Unlike Zapier or Make.com, n8n can be self-hosted for complete data control, handles complex branching logic, and connects to virtually any API through its extensive node library or custom HTTP requests.

I build n8n workflows that handle everything from simple data syncing between two apps to multi-step business processes involving conditional logic, error handling, external API calls, and database operations. If data moves between two places in your business manually, n8n can likely automate it.

n8n runs 24/7. It doesn't forget, doesn't get tired, and doesn't need to be reminded. Once built, your workflows just work, silently handling the operational backbone of your business.

How It Works

From scattered apps to a connected system

A structured approach to building workflows that are reliable, maintainable, and genuinely useful.

01: Audit

Map your data flows

I audit where data enters your business, where it needs to go, and what transformations it requires along the way. This surfaces the highest-value automation opportunities and reveals hidden inefficiencies in your current stack.

02: Build

Build and test rigorously

I build the n8n workflow with full error handling, logging, and edge-case coverage. Every node is tested with real data, including failure scenarios. You get documentation covering what the workflow does, what triggers it, and how to maintain it.

03: Automate

Deploy and monitor

The workflow goes live on your n8n instance (or I can set one up for you). I monitor the first production runs, handle any edge cases that emerge, and hand over a system that runs reliably without daily babysitting.
