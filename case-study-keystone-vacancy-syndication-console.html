The brief

Keystone's leasing team was manually posting vacant units to Zillow, Apartments.com, Facebook Marketplace, and 9 other portals — a process that took 4 hours per listing. They needed it done with one click.

02 /

The plan I drafted

  • Enter each listing once
  • Publish it to every portal automatically
  • Bring every enquiry back into one inbox
  • Show which portals actually produce tenants
03 /

The process, step by step

Listing management console with a vacancy entry form, 12-portal syndication toggle dashboard, real-time publish status per portal, lead inbox aggregating enquiries from all portals, and a leasing analytics dashboard showing days-on-market and enquiry sources.

  1. Listing Entry Interface

    Single form captures all listing data (address, rent, beds/baths, amenities, photos). One entry, no duplication. Listings stored in Airtable as the single source of truth.

  2. Portal Syndication

    n8n workflow posts to 12 portals via their respective APIs and form-submission automation. Portal-specific formatting handled per destination.

  3. Lead Aggregation

    All enquiry replies from every portal flow into a unified inbox in the console. Each lead is tagged by source, property, and enquiry type.

  4. Analytics Dashboard

    Days-on-market tracker, enquiry volume by portal, lead-to-showing conversion rate, and cost-per-lead by source.

04 /

The bottleneck

! bottleneckEvery portal wants different data

Each listing portal uses its own fields, formats and limits, so one listing can't simply be copied everywhere.

05 /

How I fixed it

✓ fix

A portal-specific mapping step reshapes the single listing into each portal's format before posting, so the manager still only fills in one form.

06 /

The result

  • Per-listing posting time reduced from 4 hours to 8 minutes
  • All 12 portals populated simultaneously — vs. manual posting to 3-4 portals previously
  • Average days-on-market reduced from 22 days to 11 days after full-portal syndication
