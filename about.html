My name is Wisdom, and I'm an AI developer, automation engineer, and web specialist based in Nigeria. I work with entrepreneurs, agencies, and growing businesses across the globe — helping them scale by building AI-powered systems, high-converting websites, intelligent automation workflows, and data-driven lead generation pipelines.

My work spans five core disciplines: AI development using Claude Code to build custom agents, intelligent tools, and document pipelines; AI automation with n8n, Make.com, and Zapier to eliminate manual work; AI-powered websites that actively qualify leads and close deals 24/7; custom web development with Shopify, Webflow, and vanilla JavaScript; and lead generation using OpenClaw data extraction and GoHighLevel CRM to fill pipelines with qualified prospects.

I started freelancing because I saw a gap: most business owners knew these technologies were possible but couldn't find someone who spoke both their language and the language of technology. I translate business problems into technical solutions — clearly, honestly, and without the jargon.

Over 50 projects and clients in 8+ countries later, the thread is consistent: systems that work harder than any hire, websites that convert while you sleep, and pipelines that deliver qualified leads on demand. Every project starts with the same question: what does success actually look like for you?

I hold Make Academy certifications at the Foundation, Basics, and Advanced levels, and I maintain a 100% Job Success Score on Upwork. These numbers matter — but what matters more is that clients come back, and they send their network.
