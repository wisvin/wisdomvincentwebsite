What is Zapier?

The world's largest automation platform, connected to everything you use

Zapier connects over 6,000 apps — from Gmail and Slack to Salesforce, Shopify, and everything in between. A "Zap" is a trigger-and-action workflow: when something happens in one app, Zapier automatically takes action in another.

I build multi-step Zapier automations with filters, formatters, paths, delays, and custom logic that go far beyond basic two-step Zaps. Whether it's lead routing, CRM updates, invoice creation, or content scheduling, I design Zaps that handle the full complexity of your real business workflow.

The result: hours saved every week, zero data falling through the cracks, and a business that responds faster without adding headcount.

How It Works

From manual tasks to automated systems

A clear three-phase build process that turns repetitive work into a Zap that runs itself.

01: Audit

Map the manual work

We identify every repetitive, copy-paste, or "don't forget to" task in your workflow. I map the triggers, the data, and the desired outcomes — so the Zap is built around real work, not assumptions.

02: Build

Engineer the Zap

I build and configure the full automation — multi-step flows, filters, paths, formatters, and error handling. Every Zap is tested thoroughly before it touches your live data.

03: Monitor

Hand over and optimise

Once live, I walk you through the Zap, monitor the first runs, and refine any edge cases. You get full ownership of the automation — and a system that keeps getting better.
