Privacy policy

Plain-English answers to what happens with the information you share here. Last updated 30 September 2026.

Who I am

This website belongs to Wisdom, a freelance AI developer and automation engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with clients worldwide. For anything privacy-related, email wvemofficial@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, the service you need, your budget range and your message.
  • Free audit form: your name, email and (optionally) your business or website.
  • Booking a call: handled by Calendly, which collects the details you enter there.
  • Direct messages: anything you send by email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Upwork.

Forms are delivered to me through Formspree. I never ask for payment details on this site.

How I use it

Only to reply to you, prepare proposals and audits you asked for, and deliver the work we agree on. I don’t sell, rent or share your details for anyone else’s marketing, and I don’t add you to newsletters without asking.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count visits and measure page speed. They don’t use cookies and don’t identify you personally — I only see totals such as page views and which buttons were clicked. The site stores one small flag in your browser’s session storage so the intro screen shows only once per visit. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

Some features load from other providers, each with its own privacy policy: Google Fonts (typefaces), Calendly (booking), Formspree (forms), and links out to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Upwork and Credly. When you use those services, their policies apply.

How long I keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we’re in conversation or working together, and for a reasonable period afterwards for records. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Wherever you are — including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and Nigeria’s Data Protection Act — you can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email wvemofficial@gmail.com and I’ll respond within 30 days.

Client work and confidentiality

Data I handle while building systems for clients is covered by our project agreement or NDA, not this page. I only access what the work needs and never reuse client data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
