What the client came with

Leads from paid ads were landing in a spreadsheet. Follow-up depended on whoever remembered. Most leads never heard back within the first hour — the window where they are most likely to convert. Over 60% went cold. The ad spend was being wasted.

02 /

The plan I drafted

  • Capture every ad lead into GoHighLevel instantly — no spreadsheet
  • Fire a 5-touch SMS and email sequence within 5 minutes, day or night
  • Track every lead by pipeline stage so nothing falls through
  • Hand sales only the leads who are warmed up and ready to book
03 /

The process, step by step

Every new lead now enters a 5-touch SMS and email sequence that fires within 5 minutes of capture, around the clock. The pipeline tracks every contact by stage. The sales team only speaks to leads that have already been warmed up and are ready to book.

  1. Instant capture

    Ad forms push leads straight into GoHighLevel as contacts, tagged by source.

  2. 5-minute first touch

    A workflow sends the first SMS within 5 minutes of capture, followed by an email.

  3. 5-touch nurture

    Further SMS and email touches are spaced out to keep the lead warm without spamming.

  4. Pipeline tracking

    Each lead moves through defined stages, so the team can see exactly where everyone is.

  5. Booking hand-off

    When a lead books, sales is notified and the lead leaves the nurture sequence.

04 /

The bottleneck

! bottleneckLeads kept getting messages after replying

Leads who replied or booked could still receive the next automated touch, which felt robotic.

05 /

How I fixed it

✓ fix

I set replies and bookings as goal events that immediately remove the lead from the sequence and move them to the right pipeline stage.
